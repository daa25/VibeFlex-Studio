# WELLO development boundary

WELLO runs as a separate brand and remains secondary to restoring the existing
VibeFlex Sports and 490 Movement operating connections.

## Initial scope

- Premium scrub concept presentation: Look Good. Feel Great. Do Work.
- Six collection concepts, retaining the supplied lookbook as the design source.
- Dental team interest and professional product evaluation workflow.
- Dr. Jacob Berger questionnaire and evaluation materials separate from the
  customer-facing brand presentation.
- Prototype feedback on fit, collar pen storage, utility pockets, hidden zippers,
  comfort and wash durability.

Fabric benefits remain design goals until validated. Interest collection must
not be presented as available inventory or a functioning checkout.

## Connection rules

Reuse the verified development tooling and setup process. Give WELLO its own
brand records and lead collection. Establish its store and fulfillment accounts
before attaching payment credentials, creating purchasable products, or routing
orders. Do not route scrub purchases through the existing POD T-shirt pipeline.

Current status: development scope prepared; no separate WELLO repository,
hosting project, store, payment connection or production checkout is provisioned
by this change.
